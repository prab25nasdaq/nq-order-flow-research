Why order flow, not just trade flow

A footprint chart -- however detailed -- can only show where trades executed. It has no visibility into resting orders that were placed and then pulled before ever trading. Some of the most information-dense behavior in a market happens exactly there: size building at a level as price approaches it, then vanishing right before contact -- liquidity that shaped the outcome without ever printing a single trade. Because Book-Flow Level Candles are built from raw depth and quote updates rather than trade prints alone, that entire category of behavior becomes visible, at the resolution of every price level, in every bar.

Volume bars, not time bars

Each bar closes after a fixed amount of executed volume, not a fixed clock interval -- a more honest, apples-to-apples unit of "how much happened," instead of two wildly different market states wearing the same 1-minute label.

Resting liquidity, not just trades

Every bid and ask addition and withdrawal at every price level is recorded -- not just where trades printed -- so liquidity that shaped price without ever executing is no longer invisible.

Bid and ask, kept separate

Two bars that look identical on a traditional chart -- same open, same close, same green candle -- can look completely different here, because buying pressure and the other side simply stepping aside are no longer collapsed into one number.

Live book, same axis

The current resting order book renders on the identical price axis as the historical flow record, so where liquidity concentrated historically and where it's sitting right now are one glance apart, not two separate tools.
